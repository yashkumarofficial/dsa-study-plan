The rule we follow

First we do the basic and easy problems of all topics. Only then do we move to medium and hard.

The number next to each item is its S. No. inside its chapter. Solve them in that order, top to bottom. A Learn tag means a list to study and understand; a Solve tag means problems to code.

Where this comes from

This plan follows the GeeksforGeeks DSA tutorial and roadmap, as it is.

Chapters, topics and problems come from the GeeksforGeeks list in its own order. We only fixed spellings, added serial numbers, and split the list into two phases so we do the easier problems of every topic first. Each item opens a GeeksforGeeks search for that topic.
